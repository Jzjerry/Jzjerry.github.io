Biography

I am a Ph.D. student in Microelectronics at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), advised by Prof. Yangdi Lyu. I received my M.Phil. degree in Microelectronics from HKUST(GZ) in 2024 and my B.S. degree in Electronic Information Science and Technology from Central South University in 2022.

My research focuses on efficient computing systems for edge AI, spanning TinyML, hardware/software co-design and design space exploration of microprocessors and accelerators. This blog is where I record my studies & ideas, and sometimes, life as well.

Research Interests

  • Tiny Machine Learning
  • Hardware/Software Co-design
  • Design Space Exploration
  • High-Level Hardware Design
